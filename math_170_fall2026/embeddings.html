One-hot can’t say “alike” embeddings · cosine

You are the AI. Before you can compute with words you have to turn them into numbers. Here are the two classic ways — one where every word is a stranger to every other word, and one where the map itself knows that cats and dogs go together.

the worksheet’s 5-word sentence → 5 one-hot rows

u·v and the lengths ‖u‖, ‖v‖ are exactly the dot product and vector length you already know. Dividing the dot product by both lengths cancels how long the arrows are, so the only thing left is direction: cos θ = −1 opposite · 0 unrelated (a right angle) · +1 same direction. That is why two words can be far apart on the map yet still point the same way — cosine reads the angle, not the distance.
