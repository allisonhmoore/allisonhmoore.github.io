8 activities

What type of learning is it?

  • Learning-Type Sorter
    Sort the worksheet's scenarios into supervised / unsupervised / reinforcement — then flip a "what if" switch and watch the right answer change.
  • You Are the Machine: Sandwich Classifier
    The 20-row sandwich table three ways: numbers only → with labels → with labels and item names.

Points, lines and regions

  • One Line, Four Ways
    Six word problems demonstrated as a graph, a formula, a table of values, and a plain sentence with real units. Adjust one format and observe the others change accordingly
  • Draw the Decision Line
    Separate healthy from not-healthy sandwiches with a line — then state the inequality and count your misses.

Images become vectors

  • Grid Digits → Binary Vectors → L1
    The scary Σ formula as fifteen tiny same-or-different questions, tallied one cell at a time.
  • Trainable Machine native
    A self-hosted stand-in for Teachable Machine: draw examples, train, test on held-back data, read the accuracy matrix — and see exactly why it decided.

Distance is a choice

  • Three Rulers on One Grid
    Two points, three answers: walk the grid (L1), cut straight across (L2), or take the biggest single step (L∞). Then switch modes and see the circle each ruler draws — a diamond, a circle, a square.
  • Nearest Neighbors Playground
    Drag anything — squares, circles, the star. Change k, switch rulers. The vote, and the answer, moves.

The Shared Plane

One coordinate plane on the projector; every student is a named cursor on it from their own device. Three modes: Cluster Yourselves, Human Coordinate Plane, and Rate & Cluster Movies. It's the one piece that needs a server — see classroom/README.md to run it (FastAPI, one process, no database).
