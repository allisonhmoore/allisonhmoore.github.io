Build me this graph special graphs

A graph is vertices plus edges — nothing else. You will be asked for a particular graph by name. Build it on the board: tap empty space for a vertex, drag from one vertex to another for an edge. It also asks you to read what you drew — order, size, Δ, δ and the degree sequence are yours to count, not the board's to announce. When you think both are right, press Check my work at the bottom: it judges the graph and your five numbers in one go, it does not accept near misses, and the point needs both.

Read your own graph

The board will not tell you any of these. Count them off the picture you drew, type them in, and check yourself. A right box turns gold; a wrong one goes dashed and tells you what to count — never the number; an empty one waits, dotted, until you fill it. They are marked against the graph you drew, so you can read a wrong graph correctly. Change the board and the marks clear, because the answers did.

Write the degree sequence with a comma or a space between the numbers, one number for every vertex. Order does not matter — it is compared sorted, largest first, which is how the worksheets write it.

Try: build the graph, fill in all five boxes, then press Check my work — one press marks both. If your degree sequence and the commission's disagree, the graph is wrong — not the boxes.
